03 Approach

How an engagement moves.

A short arc, repeated until the number is real. Strategy first, then the operating.

  1. 01

    Listen

    The constraint comes first: margin, forecast, and the customer. Channels are a consequence.

  2. 02

    Frame

    Positioning, the offer, and the number the work has to move. One story the deck and the site can share.

  3. 03

    Build

    The mix, the pages, the pipeline, the creative. Product narrative and revenue operations in the same plan.

  4. 04

    Stay

    Reporting, enablement, and a cadence the team can keep. Then iterate where the market answers.

JP is open to fractional and contract work, and to in-house roles when a team wants the same person on the strategy and the system. The sectors she knows from the inside are marketplace, AI, SaaS, and e-commerce.

Strategy first. Then stay long enough to run it.

04 The practice

Cocomailo is a person, not a floor of account managers.

JP Camacho has spent more than a decade as a marketing, revenue, and product leader. She works across marketplace, AI, SaaS, and commerce — from the forecast and the channel mix to the moment a customer buys. Across those businesses, from seed to scale, she has generated over nine figures.

The practice is Coco Mailo, opened in 2024. This site calls it Cocomailo. She takes fractional chief-marketing roles, and growth, product, and revenue leadership beside them. Engagements are not tied to one city.

She works in English and Tagalog. She studied advertising at St. Clair College, the public college in Windsor, finishing on the academic honour roll. In 2004 she won gold in marketing at the student competition run by the Advertising Educators Association of Canada. She interned as a junior copywriter at Holmes & Lee, a Toronto advertising agency, and at MacLaren McCann, then one of Canada’s large Toronto agencies. She describes that stint as pitch and copywriting work, including for Second Cup, GM Canada, Tylenol, and West 49 — not accounts the practice holds.

The values she names for the work: servant leadership, elegant solutions, radical change, a builder’s mindset, and a standard of excellence. She is a mentor, and an advocate for BIPOC and 2SLGBTQ+ communities. She has also volunteered with Blocks Recording Club, a Toronto co-operative of independent musicians, and led a volunteer coffee team at ideacity, the Toronto ideas conference started by Moses Znaimer.

05 Record

The years before Coco Mailo.

In-house leadership. The figures belong to those roles.

  1. 2022 — 2025

    Flexday

    Head of Marketing and Product · Toronto

    Flexday is a Toronto marketplace where companies book private offices and meeting rooms by the day or month, and landlords can earn from vacant space. JP led growth, product, and brand. In her record, demand grew 4–6× and customers and revenue doubled. She reported to the CEO and managed three contractors, including guidance on grants and the P&L.

  2. 2015 — 2021

    FlipGive

    Digital Marketing Director, then Senior Director, then VP of Merchandising · Toronto

    FlipGive lets teams, schools, and community groups raise money when people shop online or buy gift cards, with a share of the purchase returned to the group. JP rose from digital marketing director to VP of merchandising, built a department of six, and grew a merchant supply to about 700, among them Amazon, Walmart, Apple, Sephora, Starbucks, and Loblaw’s. In her record, gross merchandise value grew about 150×, to roughly $100 million, revenue grew 60×, and premium-segment retention reached 98%.

  3. 2012 — 2015

    Umbra

    Digital Marketing Specialist · Toronto

    Umbra is a Toronto housewares and home-design company, founded in 1979, with products sold around the world. JP led e-commerce and digital brand communications. Website sales rose 20%, to $3 million, and search returned 150% on its spend. She supervised a small specialist team, project-managed a Magento rebuild, and was the in-house digital lead for Umbra Shift, the company’s higher-end line.

  4. 2009 — 2012

    iSkin

    Internet Marketing Coordinator, then interim Marketing Manager

    iSkin is a Toronto-born maker of protective cases and accessories for phones and other mobile devices, and has long exhibited at CES. JP moved from internet marketing coordinator to interim marketing manager. Website sales rose 17%, to $10 million. She planned two major trade shows, including CES in Las Vegas, and kept launches aligned across PR, digital, and wholesale.

  5. 2007 — 2008

    Starbucks Coffee Canada

    Marketing Coordinator, Eastern Canada

    The Canadian business of the coffee retailer. JP coordinated new-store, local, and product marketing in Eastern Canada during a large expansion: more than 90 store openings, promotional periods, and product rollouts, plus developed-market work that included a TIFF sponsorship.
